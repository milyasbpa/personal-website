## Systems thinking for language acquisition

When I started learning Japanese (日本語), I treated it like memorizing syntax. I tried reading textbooks cover-to-cover and drilling flashcards without context. It didn't work.

What changed my trajectory was applying systems thinking: treating language acquisition as an input-output pipeline with measurable feedback loops.

## Combining Anki with native reading

Spaced repetition systems (SRS) like Anki are powerful, but only when paired with actual sentence mining. Every time I encounter a new word in technical documentation or articles, I extract the full sentence rather than isolated vocabulary.
